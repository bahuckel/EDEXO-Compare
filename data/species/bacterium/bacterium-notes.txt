Key extraction notes (this one is IMPORTANT)
This is the most complex genus so far:
Multi-layer logic: atmosphere → volcanism → color system
Universal rule:
Must have thin atmosphere (no exceptions)
Volcanism is critical (don’t miss this in filtering):
Some species will NOT spawn without it (omentum, scopulum, verrata, tela)
Two different color systems exist:
Material-based (most species)
Star-based (rare cases like cerbrus, alcyoneum)
There’s even a hidden edge case:
Bacterium tela → can spawn in any atmosphere BUT requires volcanism
