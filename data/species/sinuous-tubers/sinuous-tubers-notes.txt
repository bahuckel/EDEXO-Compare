Sinuous Tubers are purely airless-world organisms, making them fundamentally different from all previous genera you modeled.
Their classification is volcanism-driven, not atmosphere-driven, which is why species overlap across planet types is common.
Each “color variant” is actually a mineralogical expression of subsurface chemistry, not a biological trait.
These organisms are effectively a surface fungal projection system of a deeper planetary organism, meaning what you scan is only a fraction of the whole entity.
The genus is heavily cluster-dependent, but unlike Osseus, it is driven by geological heat zones rather than terrain elevation.
Multiple species can coexist on a single planet due to localized volcanism variation, which is why codex entries often differ per site.
Roseum is the most geologically specific variant (silicate magma locked), while others are broader and more flexible.
The genus is one of the most bug-sensitive in codex tracking, meaning real in-game distribution can appear inconsistent due to overlapping volcanic zones.
