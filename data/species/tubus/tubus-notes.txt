Tubus is one of the most physically constrained genera in Elite Dangerous, requiring both:
extremely low gravity (≤ 0.15g)
tight temperature band (160–190K)
Unlike most flora, Tubus behaves like a biological engineering structure, not just an organism.
The internal “chimney system” suggests a self-regulating atmospheric circulation mechanism, making it closer to a living tower than a plant.
Species differences are mainly defined by:
atmosphere type (CO₂ vs Ammonia)
structural segmentation style
Tubus sororibus is the only High Metal Content–exclusive species, making it the most geologically constrained.
The genus strongly prefers flat terrain, meaning mountainous regions are usually poor spawn candidates.
Growth rings (in some species) imply long-lived organisms with measurable evolutionary age layers, which is rare among Elite flora.
Because of its height and structure, Tubus is often visually detectable from orbit-level approach compared to other genera.
Despite its size, it remains low density in distribution, which is why sample distance is unusually high (800m).
