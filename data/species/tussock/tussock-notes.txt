Tussock is one of the most widespread and high-density genera in Elite Dangerous, often acting as a “baseline bio layer” across many planets.
It is atmosphere-first, but heavily refined by temperature bands inside CO₂ environments, making it more complex than it appears.
Many species are visually similar, meaning explorers often rely on DSS heatmaps rather than direct observation.
Tussock often creates the impression of “empty planets with only bacteria”, because it blends into terrain extremely well.
The genus is heavily flat-terrain biased, but can extend into gentle slopes and mountain bases.
CO₂ species show a full temperature ladder system (145K → 195K), making it one of the most structured thermal progressions in your dataset so far.
Some species (like capillum) are infamous for low visibility + high density, making them easy to overlook even on populated biological worlds.
Despite being “common,” Tussock is one of the most mechanically important genera for exobiology farming loops due to predictable distribution.
