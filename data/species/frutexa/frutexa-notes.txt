Things that are good to know
Frutexa is atmosphere-driven, meaning filtering starts with atmosphere first, not species selection.
Temperature is a secondary constraint, only relevant for CO₂-based species.
Only Frutexa metallicum can appear on High Metal Content worlds.
All species share a strict 0.27g gravity cap, regardless of variation.
The genus uses a hybrid logic model:
deterministic mapping (atmosphere → candidates)
conditional filters (temperature + planet type)
Color is completely decoupled from species identity and depends only on parent star type, ensuring visual consistency across all variants.
Rocky worlds act as the baseline environment, even when extra planet types are allowed.
