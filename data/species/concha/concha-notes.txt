Key extraction notes (important nuances here)
Atmosphere is the main split (very clean mapping):
Ammonia → aureolas
Nitrogen → biconcavis
CO₂ → labiata + renibus
Water → renibus only
Temperature logic is subtle (easy to mess up):
labiata → ONLY <190K
renibus → 180–195K BUT ONLY for CO₂
renibus (water) → NO temp restriction listed
Mixed color systems (important for your engine):
Star-based → aureolas, labiata
Material-based → biconcavis, renibus
Edge-case standout:
Concha renibus = multi-atmosphere species (rare pattern)
