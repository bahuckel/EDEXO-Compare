WHY THIS LICENCE - DATA DERIVED FROM EDASTRO

Part of the data is derived from content published by Elite Dangerous
Astrometrics (EDAstro, https://edastro.com), made by CMDR Orvidius, which is
licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Those files are adaptations of EDAstro's
content and must carry the same licence; the rest of the data carries it too,
so the data has one licence. The EDAstro-derived files, and what was changed
(not every release contains every file):

  * data/exomastery/region-species.json
      How often each species is recorded in each galactic region, counted
      from EDAstro's codex-data.csv.
  * data/exomastery/sector-map.json
      Sector names from EDAstro's sector list, placed on a 1,280 ly grid.
      (The names themselves are the game's.)
  * data/exomastery/spatial-catalogue.json
      Nebula coordinates from EDAstro's nebulae-coordinates.csv, and Guardian
      site coordinates from EDAstro's edsmPOI.csv.
  * data/rarity/extra/edastro-codex-*.json
      Per species, how many systems have it logged, galaxy-wide and per
      region, counted from EDAstro's codex-data.csv - only the species and
      system pairs EDSM's codex dump does not have.
  * data/rarity/body-share.json
      The rarity tiers: the share of bio bodies of a species' planet types
      that carry it, computed from Spansh's galaxy dump together with EDSM's
      and EDAstro's codex data.
  * src/shared/nspModelData.ts (from version 1.2.7)
      Averages per galactic region and per main-star type for the notable
      stellar phenomena prediction, fitted on EDAstro's codex-data.csv. No
      system of EDAstro's is in it.
  * data/galaxy/bio-index.bin (a separate download from the galaxy-index
    release, not in the repository)
      The galaxy map's index of systems with recorded biology: the species
      EDAstro's codex-data.csv has in each system, joined with the systems
      Spansh's galaxy dump shows with biological signals.
  * data/exomastery/genus-body-split.json
      Per Sinuous Tubers and Brain Tree species, counts of planet type,
      volcanism, temperature band and atmosphere on the Spansh galaxy dump's
      bodies where EDSM's and EDAstro's codex log exactly one species of the
      genus in the system. No system of EDAstro's is in it.

The other sources of the data are credited in NOTICE.md.
