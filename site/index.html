What it does

Reads your live journal folder (and related game files where applicable), merges scans and organic signals, and surfaces candidate exobiology species, payouts, and route/fuel hints in a single HUD-style UI.

Local-first

Runs on your machine; data stays under your control. Pair the web UI with the desktop launcher or CLI build.
